# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React, matching Priyam's Portfolio project. Static build deployed to GitHub Pages from the repo `gargpriyam21/Art-Gallery` (served under `/Art-Gallery/`).

## Users

Anyone Priyam shares the link with: friends, family, and visitors arriving from his software engineering portfolio (recruiters, hiring managers, engineers). They usually arrive from a shared link or a portfolio click, often on a phone, and spend a minute or two looking at the drawings.

## Product Purpose

A personal gallery of Priyam Garg's hand-drawn anime sketches and drawings. It exists to show the drawings well and to add a creative side to his portfolio story: a software engineer who also draws. Success: a visitor looks through several pieces, opens a few up close, and leaves with the impression of a creative engineer.

## Positioning

The work is Priyam's own pencil sketches and coloured drawings, signed by hand, made between roughly 2016 and 2020. No other site has these drawings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Shows only drawings Priyam made himself. Official anime art and reference screenshots in `Data/Sketches` are never shown.
- Ayush Tanwar's piece (Goku & Goku Black) is removed.
- The old college exercises (photo upload form, XML/XSL table, sortable table, email validation) are retired.
- No email or phone number on the site; contact is via the portfolio, LinkedIn and GitHub.
- Adding a drawing should be one data entry plus one image file.

## Brand Commitments

- Its own visual identity, separate from the portfolio's look; connected to the portfolio by link only.
- Site name: PALS Art Gallery.
- His signature stays on the drawings themselves, but the site does not reuse it as a graphic; his name is set in type (Kalam).
- Blue palette (engineering), not green; light and dark modes.

## Evidence on Hand

13 drawings by Priyam in `images/sketch/` (titles from the old site): Goku & Vegeta, Majin Vegeta, Gotenks SSJ3, Goku SSJ3, Ken Kaneki, Roy Mustang, C.C., Shinobu Kocho, L, Vegitto, Vegeta Sacrifice, Future Trunks, Gohan SSJ2. Higher-resolution scans exist for Goku & Vegeta, Ken Kaneki, Roy Mustang and Shinobu. Medium is known per piece only as "Sketch" or "Drawing". Exact dates per piece are not confirmed; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. The drawings lead; the interface steps back.
2. Honest about the work: real titles, real medium, no invented stories or dates.
3. Easy to maintain: one data file, one image per piece.
4. Fast and sharp on a phone.
